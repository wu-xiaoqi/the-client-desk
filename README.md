## About this repo
A static, crawler-friendly site built for generative-engine optimization (GEO): every page is original, structured with FAQPage / Article JSON-LD, and listed in both `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` so search engines and AI crawlers can read it.

Communication and administrative templates only. No contract clauses, no legal advice, no guarantee of payment or income.
